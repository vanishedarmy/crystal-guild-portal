# Combat Card Hierarchy Hotfix 8

- Ability and item names remain the first, full-width line on combat cards.
- Cost or quantity is shown directly beneath the name.
- Effect, usability, or status text follows last.
- The layout guard applies to Solo Ventures, Dungeons, Expeditions, Raids, World Events, and live-stream combat controls.
- No combat values, ability costs, item behavior, schema, or protocol changed.
