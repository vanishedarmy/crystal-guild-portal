# Gold Saucer Alpha 1 — Web Hotfix 1 Visual Treatment

Web-only visual update on top of v1.6.0 Gold Saucer Alpha 1.

- Adds explicit scenic DOM image layers for the Gold Saucer lobby, Mini Cactpot, Crystal Slots, and Prize Counter.
- Replaces placeholder slot glyphs with six individual WebP symbol artworks matching the authoritative Alpha 1 slot table: Crystal, Moogle, Chocobo, Cactuar, Bomb, and Potion.
- Ships all new public artwork as WebP under `/gold-saucer/a1h1/` for cache-busting.
- No Gold Saucer odds, payouts, SP costs, daily allowance, prizes, schema, hosted sync, Portal Action protocol, or Bot behavior changes.
