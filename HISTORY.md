- **v1.1.14 — Cloud Continuity:** hosted continuity and live-state stopgates.

- **v1.1.12 — Adventure Board:** dedicated Portal adventure hub.
- **v1.1.11 — Solo Venture:** Portal-only solo adventure path.
- **v1.1.10 — Chocobo Companions:** stable and companion progression.
- **v1.1.0 — Fellowship:** shared guild progression and Portal Actions foundation.
- **v1.0.x — Ultima / Ultima Web:** first production Crystal Guild release line.
