- Attack, Ability, Item, and Defend remain available while scrolling through combat.

- Live Stream Ventures, World Events, and Raids keep ally-target selection inside the mobile drawer when needed.
- Solo Ventures, Dungeons, Expeditions, Stream Ventures, World Events, and Raids share the same quick-HUD pattern.
- Desktop combat presentation remains unchanged.
- No combat values, action behavior, schema, protocol, or player data changed.
