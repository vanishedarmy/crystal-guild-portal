# Crystal Guild v1.2.0 — Expeditions Beta 2 Hotfix 2

Hotfix 2 replaces the long-form scenic backgrounds for Solo Venture, Relics Arsenal, Blacksmith, Chocobo Stable, and Moogle Emporium with new portrait-format artwork sized to follow the full height of those tabs. It also removes the duplicated airship artwork on Guild Heroes and Player Guide while retaining the ambient page-level airship.

Beta 2 Hotfix 1 current-viewport detail-card positioning remains intact. Gameplay remains on the validated Beta 1 balance baseline.
