## Monster Identity Pass — Wave 9

Wave 9 adds ten more live Bestiary monsters to the shared portrait system while retaining the Wave 7 Image Fit Hotfix and all Wave 8 presentation rules. Every monster portrait remains a true edge-to-edge 512×768 (2:3) WebP asset.

### Wave 9 monsters

- Rift Hound
- Void Mage
- Abaddon
- Rift Dragon
