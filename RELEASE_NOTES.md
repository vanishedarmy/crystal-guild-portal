# Crystal Guild v1.2.0 — Expeditions Beta 2 Hotfix 2
## Portrait Scenic Backgrounds

- Replaces the long-form Solo Venture, Relics Arsenal, Blacksmith, Chocobo Stable, and Moogle Emporium scenic artwork with new 941×1672 portrait-format backgrounds.
- Scenic layers now span the complete height of those long tabs rather than ending after the first 300–560px scene band.
- Functional cards remain on translucent readable surfaces over the artwork.
- Removes the duplicate airship presentation on Guild Heroes and Player Guide by keeping the ambient body airship while removing the second hero/header copy.
- Camp/Recovery artwork is unchanged.

- Compatible Bot runtime remains `1.2.0-expeditions-b1`.
