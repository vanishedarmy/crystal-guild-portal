Unofficial Final Fantasy-inspired Twitch RPG

Become a hero.
Probably get stabbed.

Recruit a persistent hero in Twitch chat or directly from the Adventurer Portal, master Jobs, join random battles, hunt notorious marks, craft and forge equipment, awaken Relic weapons, and throw the entire Guild at raid bosses that were clearly somebody else's problem.

Quick Start

Your first useful ten minutes in the Guild.

Recruit. Use the Adventurer Portal's Recruit Adventurer option after signing in with Twitch, or type !recruit in chat. Both create the same persistent hero and announce the new recruit in Twitch chat.
Pick a starter Job. Use !job , then choose Warrior, Monk, Thief, Black Mage, White Mage or Red Mage.
Adventure. Use !venture . A Venture can produce an ENCOUNTER or a TREASURE CHEST. Encounter Ventures open a rally window so others can use !join; treasure Ventures immediately award a level-appropriate item. Rally Ventures are started and joined in Twitch chat with !venture and !join. The Adventurer Portal Adventure Board offers travel, Hunts, and Solo Venture, a separate website-only, player-paced mode with its own 7-minute cooldown. Each stage rolls 80% ENCOUNTER / 20% TREASURE, enemies stay roughly within Lv.-2 through Lv.+5, and a run can continue for up to three stages. Encounter rounds wait for your Attack, Ability, Item, or Defend choice and never post automatic Twitch chat announcements.
Grow. Victories award Character XP, Job JP, gil, drops and Bestiary credit.
Build the Fellowship. Normal play also earns shared Fellowship Renown and can advance community Orders, Deeds and project requirements. Use !fellowship to see the Guild's current LVL and your personal Guild Rank.
Gear up. Check the randomized Job-tailored !shop , buy by offer number with !buy 3 , then use !inventory , !gear and !equip <item> .
Recover. Damage persists. Use !rest for a free stream-aware Camp recovery (15m LIVE / 1h OFFLINE base) or !use tent to recover immediately. Eight hours without an intentional player action also grants a Long Rest, and the whole Guild is refreshed once when a new stream goes LIVE.
Branch out. Unlock regions, Hunts, advanced Jobs, crafting, Masteries and eventually Relic weapons. If you are unsure what comes next, use !guild for the command list or !next for your hero’s next objective. Aliases !cghelp and !cgcommands work too.
Golden rule: Crystal Guild has no permanent death, XP loss, equipment durability or random forge failures. Getting flattened is embarrassing, not character-ending.

Chat Command Reference

Commands are grouped by what you want to do. Search the list or choose a category.

Reading the syntax: <name> means a required value. [name] means optional. Aliases do exactly the same thing as the primary command.
Portal shortcut: Signed-in adventurers can claim their randomized Daily Guild Provisions directly from their own Overview. Each claim includes level-appropriate provisions plus Gil and uses the same 24-hour cooldown as !daily. Twitch subscribers receive tier-based Daily bonuses; use !substatus to check the currently detected tier.

Character Progression

Character Level is your permanent overall progression; Job Levels are independent tracks for each Job.

Character Level

Levels 1–99. XP requirements rise as you level. Character Level unlocks regions, Hunts, recipes, equipment and Relic stages.

Job Level

Each Job levels independently from 1–50 using JP. JP requirements rise with Job Level, and changing Jobs never erases progress.

Ability Milestones

Abilities unlock at JLv. 5, 10, 20, 30 and 40 . JLv.50 unlocks that Job's cross-Job Mastery.

Mastery

You have two Mastery slots . A mastered Job can lend its Mastery bonus while you play another Job.

!mastery warrior 1

Fellowship — Guild Progression & Commands

Fellowship is Crystal Guild's shared community progression layer. Your hero keeps all normal character progression while everyday play also helps the channel-wide Guild grow.

Two different progress tracks: the shared Fellowship is labeled LVL 1–10, while each player separately earns a personal Guild Rank. They are related, but they are not the same thing.

Shared Fellowship LVL

The whole channel shares one Fellowship LVL. Resolved Ventures, Hunts, raids, crafting, forging, achievements and other meaningful play can earn shared Renown or advance Guild objectives.

Your Guild Rank

Your personal Fellowship Renown raises your individual standing from Recruit through Private, Corporal, Sergeant and Lieutenant tiers to Captain. Use !rank to see your next promotion.

Guild Projects

The streamer selects the Guild's active work. Construction permanently improves facilities, Supply Drives are shorter Gil/material goals, and Guild Objectives advance automatically from activities such as Hunts, Expeditions, and raids. Donations are always voluntary.

Facilities

The Guild Board now centers on five major facilities: Guild Hall, Workshop, Chocobo Stables, Guild Archive, and the future Airship Dock. Major facilities use a simple Lv. 0–3 construction path. The Airship Dock is visible from the start so the Guild can see what it is working toward, but construction is not open yet.

Facility Services

Facility levels now unlock practical services. Workshop Lv. 2 opens basic material refining and Lv. 3 adds advanced refining. Chocobo Stables improve Bond XP by 5% per level, and Guild Archive research adds a small rare-event bonus to Expeditions from Lv. 2 onward.

Building Facilities

Facility projects can ask for Gil, materials, and gameplay milestones. Gil and material donations are voluntary. Activities such as Ventures, raids, forging, and monster victories fill gameplay goals automatically.

Orders & Deeds

Orders are rotating weekly community objectives. Each completed Order still awards shared Guild Renown, and players who helped with that Order now receive its listed personal Gil and Fellowship Renown reward. Clearing all three adds a one-time weekly participation bonus for everyone who helped with at least one. Deeds remain permanent Guild-wide milestones.

Founding Members

Heroes carried forward from the pre-Fellowship Guild are recognized as Founding Members. This recognition does not replace or reset any existing character progress.

Stats, Combat Values & Status Effects — Quick Guide

New to RPG stats? These are the numbers Crystal Guild uses to decide how hard you hit, how much punishment you can take, and how your Job performs in battle. Different Jobs favor different stats, so there is no single best stat for every hero.

Quick mental model: STR hits harder · VIT keeps you standing · MAG strengthens spells · SPR resists magic · DEX acts sooner · LCK improves critical hits.

STR — Strength

Raises physical damage. STR also contributes to the maximum AP pool for Jobs that use martial techniques.

VIT — Vitality

Represents physical toughness. Higher VIT reduces damage from physical enemy attacks; Jobs with stronger VIT growth also tend to develop larger HP pools.

MAG — Magic

Raises magical damage and the strength of many healing effects. MAG also contributes to the maximum MP pool.

SPR — Spirit

Represents magical resilience. Higher SPR reduces incoming magical damage and also contributes to maximum MP.

DEX — Dexterity

Helps determine turn order when heroes queue the same kind of action. DEX also contributes to the maximum AP pool.

LCK — Luck

Raises your chance to land a physical critical hit. Some Jobs, equipment and effects can add further critical-hit bonuses.

Derived Combat Values

ATK — Attack

Flat physical power supplied mainly by weapons and equipment. ATK is combined with STR when calculating physical damage.

M.PWR — Magic Power

Flat magical power supplied mainly by caster equipment. Magic Power is combined with MAG when calculating magical damage.

HP — Hit Points

Your health. Damage lowers HP; reaching 0 HP knocks the hero out until they are revived or properly recovered.

MP — Magic Points

A persistent resource spent on magical abilities. MP does not refill automatically after battle; recover it through camp, recovery items or applicable battle recovery.

AP — Action Points

A persistent resource spent on martial and physical techniques. AP does not refill automatically after battle; recover it through camp, AP items or applicable battle recovery.

Not every Job uses every value equally. A Black Mage cares far more about MAG, Magic Power and MP than STR, while a Warrior generally benefits more from STR, ATK, VIT and AP. Hybrid Jobs may use both sides of the system.

Buffs & Tactical Status Effects

Buffs are temporary combat effects applied by abilities, items, equipment effects, or enemy behavior. Their exact strength and duration depend on the source; the descriptions below explain what each status changes in Crystal Guild.

Protect

Reduces incoming damage while active. Stronger Protect effects reduce a larger share of the hit. Defend can provide an even stronger temporary reduction for that round.

Taunt

Makes that hero more likely to be selected by single-target enemy attacks and skills. It raises target-selection weight rather than guaranteeing every attack.

Evasion

Adds a chance to avoid incoming attacks entirely. Evasion from temporary status and applicable equipment effects combine, with a safety cap so a hero cannot become permanently unhittable.

Power Up

Raises outgoing damage. On enemies it increases their offensive pressure; on heroes it strengthens damage dealt while the effect lasts.

Focus

Improves physical critical-hit chance for heroes. Enemies do not make normal critical rolls, so enemy Focus instead increases their outgoing damage pressure.

Haste

Moves actions earlier in the round and increases outgoing damage pressure. Haste does not grant an extra action; enemies still take one normal action under the soft-targeting system.

Regen

Restores HP at the end of each round while the status remains active. The amount depends on the strength of the Regen effect.

Ailments & Harmful Status Effects

Ailments can be inflicted by enemy skills, player abilities, and certain equipment effects. Gear may provide resistance or immunity to specific ailments, and cleansing items can remove them during battle.

Vulnerable

Causes the target to take increased incoming damage. The bonus damage is bounded so Vulnerable cannot scale without limit.

Weaken

Reduces the target's outgoing damage. It affects both heroes and enemies, making their attacks and damaging abilities less threatening.

Slow

Moves actions later in the round and reduces outgoing damage pressure. Slow does not remove a turn; it makes that turn later and weaker.

Stun

The affected hero or enemy loses their action for the round while Stun is active. Smelling Salts can remove Stun from a hero.

Poison

Deals damage at the end of each round based on the target's maximum HP and the strength of the Poison effect. Antidote removes Poison.

Silence

Prevents a hero from using MP-based abilities. AP techniques, Attack, Defend, and applicable items remain available. Echo Herbs remove Silence.

Blind

Gives physical attacks and physical abilities a chance to miss. Magical damage is not affected by Blind. Eye Drops remove it.

Status duration: Battle status displays use entries such as Protect:2 or Poison:3; the number is the remaining rounds. Reapplying a status refreshes it using the stronger magnitude and longer remaining duration. Chronos Tear removes Slow, while Remedy and Panacea broadly clear harmful statuses.

Chocobo Companions

Your registered Chocobo is your companion for distant Expeditions.

Acquisition path: the Fellowship constructs the Chocobo Stable, then you complete A Chocobo of Your Own, purchase a 500 Gil Chocobo Registration Writ, wait one hour for it to activate, and register your bird.

Shared Stable

The Chocobo Stable is a Fellowship LVL 2 project requiring 5,000 Gil and 10 completed Ventures. Once built, it is available Guild-wide.

Personal Quest

Reach Character Lv.10, complete 10 Ventures, and record at least one C-Rank Hunt clear. Completing the quest awards 250 Gil.

Registration Writ

The Writ costs 500 Gil and takes one hour to validate. The countdown keeps its progress if you leave the Portal and come back later.

Bond Specialization

Registered Chocobos gain Bond XP and have one persistent Expedition specialty derived from their existing trait: Guardian, Scout, Pathfinder, Forager, or Treasure Hunter. The specialty improves at Bond Lv.5, 10, 15, and 20.

Expedition readiness: you need a registered Chocobo and the completed Fellowship Expedition Board. See Expeditions for the full travel guide.

Use !chocobo, !chocoboquest, !chocobowrit, !registerchocobo <name>, and !chocoboname <name>, or use the Adventurer Portal Chocobo Stable.

Expeditions

Expeditions — v1.2

Take your Chocobo beyond the normal Adventure Board on persistent, multi-stage journeys where resources, choices, exploration, and the Expedition Cache carry from stage to stage.

Unlock: the Guild must complete the Expedition Board, which becomes available at Fellowship LVL 6 + Guild Hall Tier 1. Each adventurer also needs their own registered Chocobo.

Regions: Emerald Wilds, Ashen Highlands, Mistfen Marshes, Frostveil Reach, or the Astral Scar.

Choose a Region

Emerald Wilds · Lv.1–12

Exploration-focused forests, rivers, ruins, and overgrown trails. Primary finds include Ochu Vine, Cockatrice Pinion, and Ancient Cloth.

Ashen Highlands · Lv.8–20

Combat and material gathering across mines, volcanic ravines, and old fortifications. Expect unstable terrain and harder fights.

Mistfen Marshes · Lv.15–28

Hazard-heavy wetlands and sunken ruins where route risk, status pressure, and preparation matter more.

Frostveil Reach · Lv.22–38

An endurance region of frozen passes, abandoned strongholds, and bitter conditions that put recovery resources under pressure.

The Astral Scar · Lv.30+

Late-game dimensional ruins, crystal fractures, rare encounters, and unpredictable Astral instability.

Choose a Route

Scout

3 stages. Shorter commitment with lower overall risk and a reduced reward multiplier.

Journey

5 stages. The balanced standard route for exploration and rewards.

Deep Expedition

8 stages. Longer and more dangerous, with the strongest base reward multiplier.

After completing the planned route, some Expeditions let you push deeper for up to two bonus stages. Returning instead secures what you have already earned.

What Happens During a Journey

Persistent Travel

Your travel and current stage stay saved. You can leave the Portal and return later without losing the Expedition or its pending decision.

Stage Events

Stages may become combat, treasure, resource nodes, hazards, discoveries, travelers, Chocobo events, camp opportunities, or rare encounters.

Resources Carry Forward

Your HP, MP, AP, and consumed supplies persist across the run. Expedition Camp opportunities help you recover before deciding whether to continue.

Route Choices

Some stages ask you to choose a path or response. Choices can alter danger, rewards, discoveries, and the next stage rather than acting as flavor-only prompts.

Expedition Cache, Return & Defeat

Expedition Cache

Gil, materials, and other Expedition rewards stay in a temporary Cache while you travel. They are not permanently banked until you make it home.

Safe Return

You may return to the Guild early when offered the option. A normal safe return banks the full current Expedition Cache.

Chocobo Rescue

If the run ends in defeat or combat retreat, your Chocobo brings you home and preserves most of the Cache instead of wiping the entire journey.

Exploration, Objectives & Regional Bosses

Each region tracks persistent exploration. Reaching 25%, 50%, 75%, and 100% advances its objective chain and opens progressively deeper regional content.

25% / 50%

Early and mid-region objectives establish landmarks, routes, and discoveries. Progress remains permanently recorded even between separate Expeditions.

75% · Boss Contract

The region's special Boss Expedition unlocks: Elder Ochu, Inferno Drake, Malboro Sovereign, Frost Behemoth, or Rift Wyrm.

Rare Encounters

Higher exploration can add dangerous regional variants and rarer opportunities to the normal event pool.

100% · Region Mastery

Mastery is permanent and awards the region's achievement/title plus a small regional Expedition bonus. Mastered regions remain fully playable.

Expedition Atlas & Legendary Contracts

Expedition Atlas

Your five regional surveys now combine into one overall Atlas rank. The Adventure Board shows overall exploration plus total stages, completed runs, Regional Boss clears, mastered regions, and unlocked Legendary routes.

Cartography Ranks

Overall exploration advances through Guild Scout → Trailblazer → Pathfinder → Surveyor → Cartographer. Master all five regions to earn the final Master Cartographer distinction.

Legendary Contracts

After a region reaches 100% Mastery and its Regional Boss has been defeated at least once, that region gains a repeatable Legendary Contract: five stages, stronger boss pressure, higher rewards, and doubled guaranteed regional boss materials.

All-Region Reward

Mastering all five Expedition regions unlocks the No Blank Spots Left achievement and the equippable Master Cartographer title. Your existing exploration progress counts toward this automatically.

Regional Discovery

Permanent Landmarks

Every region now contains four named landmarks that become permanently charted as exploration rises. The Expedition Atlas shows your total landmark count, while the selected region reveals descriptions for places you have already mapped.

Regional Event Pools

Normal resource, treasure, hazard, traveler, and Chocobo stages now use writing tied to the selected region. Emerald Wilds feels like overgrown exploration; Ashen Highlands leans into mines and Magitek debris; Mistfen into fog and drowned ruins; Frostveil into endurance; and the Astral Scar into unstable reality.

Rare Regional Events

Each region has three rare non-combat discoveries. These can produce stronger treasure or materials, recovery, Chocobo Bond progress, or an unusually valuable survey opportunity. Push Deeper has the best base chance to find them.

Expedition Journal

The active Board keeps the latest stage events in a compact Journal. It uses the same saved Expedition state, so leaving and returning to the Portal does not erase the story of the current run.

Route signals: Safe lowers combat and hazard pressure and has the lowest rare-event odds. Unknown stays balanced. Push Deeper increases combat, hazards, rewards, rare-event odds, and the weighting of rare regional monsters once they are unlocked.

Chocobo Specialization & Guild Stipends

Guardian · Steady Strider

Reduces Expedition hazard pressure and protects more of the Cache during Chocobo rescue. Bond tiers improve both effects.

Scout · Keen Eye

Improves discovery weighting and rare-event chances, making it the strongest companion for filling the Atlas and finding unusual regional opportunities.

Pathfinder · Fleet Feet

Reduces Expedition travel time. The bonus starts at 15% and grows gradually as the Chocobo reaches higher Bond tiers.

Forager / Treasure Hunter

Forager improves material weighting and yield; Treasure Hunter improves treasure and rare-event opportunities. Both scale naturally from existing Bond XP.

Completion stipend: Scout 2 Seals · Journey 4 · Deep Expedition 7 · Regional Boss Contract 10 · Legendary Contract 15. These are existing Hunt Seals, not a new Expedition currency, and are awarded only when the planned route is completed.

The Chocobo Stable also records completed Expeditions, Chocobo rescues, rare discoveries, Regional Boss activity, and Legendary Contract completions as shared companion accomplishments.

Important Rules

One Active Adventure

An active Expedition locks conflicting adventure activity and equipment changes until you return, are rescued, or the streamer uses an emergency clear.

Portal First

Expeditions are managed from Adventurer Portal → Adventure Board → Distant Expedition Board. Significant Portal milestones may post to Twitch only while the stream is LIVE; routine stages stay quiet.

Quick flow: choose region → choose Scout, Journey, or Deep → travel → resolve stages and route decisions → manage HP/MP/AP and Cache → return safely or push deeper → gain exploration → unlock landmarks, rare discoveries, objectives, Regional Bosses, Mastery, the Expedition Atlas, Legendary Contracts, and stronger Chocobo Bond specialties.

Combat & Stances

When a normal Venture rolls an ENCOUNTER and its rally closes, the party enters timed combat rounds. A Portal Solo Venture ENCOUNTER instead becomes a player-paced private battle: the Adventure Board shows enemy HP and intent plus your HP/MP/AP, then waits for you to choose Attack, Ability, Use Item, or Defend. One choice resolves one full round, with no Solo Venture combat timer and no automatic Twitch chat output. Rally Ventures and Hunts keep their normal timed Active Battle flow. Ordinary enemy attacks choose one hero; only designated multi-target skills can strike more than one party member.

Attack

!attack queues your Job's basic attack, including your stance, critical chance, signature attack chance and any double-strike effects.

Ability

!ability <name> [target] spends MP for magical techniques or AP for physical techniques. Current resources carry between battles. !skill and !cast are aliases.

Defend

!defend reduces incoming damage for the round and restores battle resources: 6% MP for magic users and 8% AP for physical users. Dual-resource Jobs recover both. !guard works too.

Item

!use <item> [target] can queue supported healing, MP/AP recovery, cleanse, damage, or revival items. Outside battle, field recovery items can be used on yourself normally; while the stream is LIVE you can support another adventurer with !use <item> | <username> or the Portal target field. The item is consumed only when its effect resolves.

Venture round timer: 30 seconds by default. You may replace your queued action before resolution. If you submit nothing, your hero automatically uses Attack so one AFK player cannot stall the fight.

Battle Status

!encounter shows enemy HP, combat style and statuses, the round timer, queued actions, and party HP/MP/AP.

Recent Round Log

!battlelog (aliases !roundlog and !combatlog) shows the latest resolved rounds on demand. Automatic round announcements stay short instead of flooding chat.

MP & AP

MP powers magical abilities while AP powers physical Job techniques. Normal !attack costs nothing. Current MP/AP persist between battles; camp, recovery items, and Defend restore them.

Status Effects

Protect, Taunt, Evasion, Power Up, Focus, Haste, Regen, Vulnerable, Weaken, Slow, Stun, Poison, Silence and Blind all have distinct combat effects. See Stats, Combat Values & Status Effects for the full quick guide.

KO & Revival

KO heroes stop submitting normal actions. Allies can use Raise-capable abilities or Phoenix Down to return them to the fight.

Job Abilities

Every Job has six battle abilities across JLv.1, 8, 15, 22, 30 and 40. Use !abilities or open a Job below to see their elements, costs, targets and special effects.

Enemies now fight with a plan

Combat Styles

Enemies may fight as Balanced, Aggressive, Defensive, Caster, Controller, Skirmisher or Berserker. Their style changes how often they use skills and how they react when wounded.

Named Skills

Bestiary special attacks can be used during battle and may have cooldowns. Single-target skills choose one living hero using tank/Taunt pressure, while designated party attacks such as Meteor-style skills can hit more than one hero.

Status Recovery

Antidote removes Poison, Echo Herbs remove Silence, Eye Drops remove Blind, Chronos Tear removes Slow, and Smelling Salts remove Stun. Remedy and Panacea broadly clear harmful statuses.

Enemy Info

!encounter shows the enemy combat style and current statuses. The Bestiary also records each discovered monster's battle skill.

Stances still matter

Balanced

Neutral baseline. The safe default.

Aggressive

Higher physical pressure at the cost of defense.

Defensive

Lower offense, better survival.

Magical

Favors magical damage.

Cautious

Conservative offense with improved defense.

Change stance: !stance aggressive. Use !stance without a name to inspect choices.

Joinable Active Encounters

Venture ENCOUNTERS are shared mini-events. A Venture may instead uncover a TREASURE CHEST, which awards the initiating hero an item immediately and does not open a rally.

Rally Venture Roll

!venture is a Twitch-chat activity and currently resolves as 70% ENCOUNTER or 30% TREASURE CHEST.

Treasure Chest

Chest loot is selected from roughly 5 levels below through 2 levels above the venturing hero. Consumables, weapons, armor and accessories are eligible; Relic-progression weapons are not.

Portal-only Solo Venture — Wayfarer: the Adventure Board has a private, player-paced run on a separate 7-minute cooldown. Every stage rolls 80% ENCOUNTER / 20% TREASURE, with enemy selection roughly two levels below through five levels above the hero. A run lasts up to three stages. After Stage I or II, choose Return to Guild to bank your expedition spoils or Press On for a deeper reward multiplier. Stage II adds +15% XP/JP/Gil; Stage III adds +30% and improves chest rarity weighting. XP/JP earned from defeated enemies is secured immediately, while Gil and ordinary item spoils remain at risk until you return. Defeat recovers 50% of unbanked Gil but loses loose item spoils; Retreat preserves secured progression but abandons all unbanked spoils. Fellowship Renown scales modestly with depth and remains below Rally Venture value.

Solo Battle Controls

Attack is free and reliable. Ability opens learned Job techniques with their MP/AP costs. Use Item shows eligible battle supplies and quantities. Defend reduces incoming damage for the round and restores a small amount of battle resource.

Enemy Intent

Solo battles telegraph the enemy's prepared action before you commit your turn. A dangerous named technique may make Defend, healing, control, or an aggressive finishing attempt the better choice.

Persistent Run State

HP, MP, AP, and consumed items carry through the run. A Solo Venture can wait safely for your next decision, even if you leave the Portal and return later. Guild resupply, recovery, Job changes, and equipment changes are locked until you return or retreat.

Rally reward bonus: Rally Venture encounter victories reward each participant with +5% XP, JP, and Gil per enemy level above that hero, capped at +50%. The bonus is personal to each hero's risk. Equal- or lower-level enemies give no Rally bonus, and treasure rewards are unchanged.

1. Rally

If !venture rolls an ENCOUNTER, it selects a monster and opens a 60-second rally in Twitch chat. The initiator joins automatically, and the rolled monster can be no more than ten levels above that initiating hero.

2. Join

Other eligible viewers use !join or !venture in Twitch chat. Joining consumes that hero's normal Venture cooldown.

3. Choose

Once combat starts, each living hero queues Attack, Ability, Defend, or Item. Every round resolves on its scheduled timer; queued actions never shorten the deadline.

4. Resolve & Reward

The party and monster act, HP/statuses persist to the next round, and victory awards each participant their own XP, JP, gil, Bestiary credit and loot roll.

Party roles: an ordinary enemy response chooses one living hero, with tank Jobs and Taunt drawing more target pressure. Multi-target damage only comes from enemy abilities explicitly built to hit multiple party members. Healers and support Jobs actively choose their recovery/control actions, so mitigation, healing, buffs and debuffs still make party composition matter.

Persistent HP, Camp & Recovery

Wounds persist between Ventures, Hunts and raids. KO is temporary, but you must recover before entering combat again.

Camp — Free

!rest or !camp starts a stream-aware rest: 15 minutes while the channel is LIVE and 1 hour while OFFLINE. Existing Fellowship/Infirmary Camp-time reductions can shorten those base timers. You cannot Venture, Hunt, join encounters or raid while resting. When complete, you return at full current maximum HP, MP and AP.

Tent — Immediate

!use tent consumes a Tent, clears an active camp timer and fully restores the HP, MP and AP used by your current Job, even from KO.

Other Recovery

Potion heals modest HP; Hi-Potion heals more; X-Potion restores full HP; Phoenix Down revives a KO'd hero at 30% HP.

Long Rest — 8 Hours

If you make no intentional Twitch command or Portal gameplay action for 8 hours, a Long Rest resolves automatically: full HP and the current Job's MP/AP are restored, and an ordinary Camp rest is cleared. The background game daemon checks the timer regularly, normally applying the refresh within one minute of the threshold. Passive Portal status polling does not keep the timer awake.

Stream Start Refresh

When Twitch reports a new Crystal Guild stream session as LIVE, every registered hero is refreshed to full HP and current-Job MP/AP once for that stream session. Reconnects and repeated LIVE checks do not repeat the heal.

Check Status

!hero shows your current/max HP and READY, RESTING or KO status.

Adventurer Portal: Sign in with Twitch on your own profile and open Equipment & Inventory to use eligible field recovery items or start Camp. The Portal shows a live recovery countdown without needing a page refresh. While the stream is LIVE, the Field item target control can apply eligible out-of-battle recovery items to another adventurer. Active Battle status cures remain battle actions through !use <item>.

Adventurer Portal: Sign in with Twitch on your own profile and open Jobs to switch unlocked Jobs, choose a battle stance, and equip or clear your two Mastery slots. Locked Jobs show the requirements you still need to complete.

Hunt Board

Hunts are personal timed assignments against named C/B/A/S marks. Dispatch a Hunt, let your hero handle the fight automatically, and collect the result when the rank timer expires. Hunts award XP, JP, gil and Hunt Seals, while first clears may grant unique gear, recipes or titles.

1. Dispatch

Choose an available mark on Adventurer Portal → Adventure Board → Hunt Board and press Dispatch. While the channel is LIVE, !hunt <name> can also dispatch an eligible mark.

2. Auto Hunt

No round commands are required. Your hero automatically Attacks, uses learned abilities, Defends when useful, and may spend a small number of appropriate survival items.

3. Wait

Each rank has a fixed assignment timer: C 15m, B 30m, A 2h, S 5h. You may only have one Hunt active at a time.

4. Resolve

When the timer expires, the Hunt resolves automatically. Victory records the clear and awards rewards; the result includes the mark, outcome, resources and automatic battle plan.

Repeat rewards: first clears give full rewards. Later clears award 85% XP, 85% JP, 80% gil and full Hunt Seals. Normal loot and bad-luck protection still apply.
Hunt activity: timed Hunts do not occupy the shared Rally/Active Battle slot, so other Guild members can continue their own Hunts and shared encounters while your hero is away.

Crafting

Recipes turn monster drops into useful items and equipment. Some recipes are known from the start; others must be discovered. Rare drops use bad-luck protection. Selling returns 70% of material value, 50% of gear value and 40% of consumable value.

Adventurer Portal: Open the Blacksmith tab on your own signed-in profile to compare known recipes against your live material, Gil and Hunt Seal totals, then craft directly without retyping recipe names in chat.

Blacksmith Forge

Weapons, armor and accessories can be upgraded from +0 to +5. Upgrades never fail and do not add durability.

Live Community Raids

Community boss fights use a join period, timed rounds, boss phases, enrage mechanics, persistent HP and contribution tracking.

Raid timers: the join period and combat rounds advance automatically, even if nobody sends another chat message.

Join

The streamer spawns a boss; the raid rally lasts 90 seconds . Type !raid to enter.

Round Actions

A new round begins every 60 seconds . While the timer is open, queue !attack, !ability <name> [target], !defend, or !use <item> [target]. Your latest choice replaces the earlier one; no input defaults to Attack.

Limit Break

Your personal Limit Gauge fills through actions and damage. At 100%, use !limit to arm a large Job-specific burst for the next round.

Guild Summon

The raid shares one Summon Gauge. At 100%, use Ifrit, Shiva, Ramuh, Titan, Garuda or Leviathan. Summoner/Sage may also call Bahamut.

Revive

A surviving raider can use !revive <hero> , spending their own Phoenix Down to return the target at 30% max HP.

Contribution

Damage, healing and mitigation are tracked. Use !raidstats during the encounter.

FAQ & Glossary

The short answers to the questions somebody will absolutely ask five minutes after you explain them.

Do I lose XP or gear when I get KO'd?
No. KO blocks combat until recovery, but there is no permanent death, XP loss, gear loss or durability system.
Why can't I Venture?
Common reasons: the relevant normal or Solo Venture cooldown is active, you're resting, you're KO'd, or you're already involved in the active channel encounter. Solo Venture uses its own 7-minute cooldown and does not consume the normal Venture cooldown. An active Solo Venture remains waiting on the Adventure Board until you act, return, or retreat.
Does changing Jobs reset anything?
No. Character Level persists and every Job keeps its own JP/Job Level. Your current HP is clamped to the new Job's valid maximum.
What is JP?
Job Points . JP levels the Job you are currently using. Job Levels unlock passive abilities and a Mastery at JLv.50.
What are Hunt Seals?
A Hunt currency earned primarily from named marks. Spend them at !sealshop and on some crafting/Relic costs.
How does bad-luck protection work?
Some rare drops become guaranteed after enough unsuccessful attempts. When the item finally drops, that protection resets.
What is the difference between Character Level and Job Level?
Character Level (1–99) is permanent overall power and unlock progression. Job Levels (1–50 each) are independent training tracks for specific Jobs.
Can I join an encounter if I didn't start it?
Yes. During the encounter rally use !join or !venture . Joining consumes your own Venture cooldown.
Can I instantly heal instead of camping?
Yes. A Tent restores full HP and ends camp immediately. X-Potion also fully heals; Phoenix Down revives from KO.
What do boss tiers mean?
Primal Boss → World Boss → High Boss → Superboss → Ultimate Superboss. Higher tiers generally expect stronger guilds, larger HP pools and harsher raid tuning.
