Unofficial Final Fantasy-inspired Twitch RPG

Become a hero.
Probably get stabbed.

Recruit a persistent hero in Twitch chat or directly from the Adventurer Portal, master Jobs, join random battles, hunt notorious marks, craft and forge equipment, awaken Relic weapons, spend a little downtime in the Gold Saucer, and throw the entire Guild at raid bosses that were clearly somebody else's problem.

Quick Start

Your first useful ten minutes in the Guild.

Recruit. Use the Adventurer Portal's Recruit Adventurer option after signing in with Twitch, or type !recruit in chat. Both create the same persistent hero and announce the new recruit in Twitch chat.
Pick a starter Job. Use !job , then choose Warrior, Monk, Thief, Black Mage, White Mage or Red Mage.
Adventure. Use !venture . A Stream Venture always produces an ENCOUNTER and opens a rally window so others can use !join. Stream Ventures still begin in Twitch chat with !venture. Their live status appears under Adventurer Portal → Stream Activities, where a signed-in adventurer can join the rally and queue Attack, Ability, Defend, or owned battle items directly from the Portal; the familiar !join, !attack, !ability, !defend, and !use chat commands remain supported. Trials and Raids use the same Portal control deck once a live rally is active. Treasure rolls remain part of the separate Portal Solo Venture mode. The Adventure Board stays focused on off-stream play with travel, Hunts, Dungeons, Expeditions, Camp, and Solo Venture, a website-only, player-paced mode with its own 7-minute cooldown. Each stage rolls 80% ENCOUNTER / 20% TREASURE, enemies stay roughly within Lv.-2 through Lv.+5, and a run can continue for up to three stages. Encounter rounds wait for your Attack, Ability, Item, or Defend choice and never post automatic Twitch chat announcements.
Grow. Victories award Character XP, Job JP, gil, drops and Bestiary credit.
Build the Fellowship. Normal play also earns shared Fellowship Renown and can advance community Orders, Deeds and project requirements. Use !fellowship to see the Guild's current LVL and your personal Guild Rank.
Gear up. Check the randomized Job-tailored !shop , buy by offer number with !buy 3 , then use !inventory , !gear and !equip <item> .
Recover. Damage persists. Use !rest for a free stream-aware Camp recovery (15m LIVE / 1h OFFLINE base) or !use tent to recover immediately. Eight hours without an intentional player action also grants a Long Rest, and the whole Guild is refreshed once when a new stream goes LIVE.
Branch out. Unlock regions, Hunts, advanced Jobs, crafting, Masteries and eventually Relic weapons. If you are unsure what comes next, use !guild for the command list or !next for your hero’s next objective. Aliases !cghelp and !cgcommands work too.
Golden rule: Crystal Guild has no permanent death, XP loss, equipment durability or random forge failures. Getting flattened is embarrassing, not character-ending.

Chat Command Reference

Commands are grouped by what you want to do. Search the list or choose a category.

Reading the syntax: <name> means a required value. [name] means optional. Aliases do exactly the same thing as the primary command.
Portal shortcut: Signed-in adventurers can claim their randomized Daily Guild Provisions directly from their own Overview. Each claim includes level-appropriate provisions plus Gil and uses the same 24-hour cooldown as !daily. Twitch subscribers receive tier-based Daily bonuses; use !substatus to check the currently detected tier.

Character Progression

Character Level is your permanent overall progression; Job Levels are independent tracks for each Job.

Character Level

Levels 1–99. XP requirements rise as you level. Character Level unlocks regions, Hunts, recipes, equipment and Relic stages.

Job Level

Each Job levels independently from 1–50 using JP. JP requirements rise with Job Level, and changing Jobs never erases progress.

Ability Milestones

Abilities unlock at JLv. 5, 10, 20, 30 and 40 . JLv. 50 unlocks that Job's cross-Job Mastery.

Mastery

You have two Mastery slots . A mastered Job can lend its Mastery bonus while you play another Job.

!mastery warrior 1

Fellowship — Guild Progression & Commands

Fellowship is Crystal Guild's shared community progression layer. Your hero keeps all normal character progression while everyday play also helps the channel-wide Guild grow.

Two different progress tracks: the shared Fellowship is labeled LVL 1–10, while each player separately earns a personal Guild Rank. They are related, but they are not the same thing.

Shared Fellowship LVL

The whole channel shares one Fellowship LVL. Resolved Ventures, Hunts, raids, crafting, forging, achievements and other meaningful play can earn shared Renown or advance Guild objectives.

Your Guild Rank

Your personal Fellowship Renown raises your individual standing from Recruit through Private, Corporal, Sergeant and Lieutenant tiers to Captain. Use !rank to see your next promotion.

Guild Projects

The streamer selects the Guild's active work. Construction permanently improves facilities, Supply Drives are shorter Gil/material goals, and Guild Objectives advance automatically from activities such as Hunts, Expeditions, and raids. Donations are always voluntary.

Facilities

The Guild Board centers on five major facilities: Guild Hall, Workshop, Chocobo Stables, Guild Archive, and Airship Dock. Major facilities use a simple Lv. 0–3 construction path. Airship Dock Lv. 1 opens construction of the Guild's shared Airship; higher Dock levels support stronger Airship upgrades.

Facility Services

Facility levels now unlock practical services. Workshop Lv. 2 opens basic material refining and Lv. 3 adds advanced refining. Chocobo Stables improve Bond XP by 5% per level, and Guild Archive research adds a small rare-event bonus to Expeditions from Lv. 2 onward.

Building Facilities

Facility projects can ask for Gil, materials, and gameplay milestones. Gil and material donations are voluntary. Activities such as Ventures, raids, forging, and monster victories fill gameplay goals automatically.

Broad milestones count every valid source. “Monsters defeated” includes Bestiary kills from normal encounters, Hunts, Ventures, Expeditions, Dungeons, and World Events. “Combat victories” includes those wins plus Raid victories. Source-specific goals such as Hunts won, Raid clears, Ventures completed, or Expeditions completed remain specific to that activity.

Guild Airship

After the Guild completes Airship Dock Lv. 1 and the Crystal Guild Airship project, the streamer can launch shared missions. Normal play supplies mission progress while the Airship is away. When it returns, a successful mission awards Fellowship Renown and pays participating heroes a modest personal reward.

Guild Legacy

Long-term Guild accomplishments record major community milestones such as facilities, donations, Expeditions, raids, Airship service, Weekly Orders and permanent Deeds. Completing one unlocks a visible Guild trophy and modest shared Renown. Use !legacy or the Fellowship page to see the Hall's current Legacy stage and next goal.

Airship Upgrades

Hull lowers mission progress pressure, Engine shortens travel time, Cargo Hold improves participant Gil rewards, Survey Array improves Guild Renown rewards, and the Crystal Core unlocks advanced missions. Upgrade tiers cannot exceed the current Airship Dock level.

Orders & Deeds

Orders are rotating weekly community objectives. Each completed Order still awards shared Guild Renown, and players who helped with that Order now receive its listed personal Gil and Fellowship Renown reward. Clearing all three adds a one-time weekly participation bonus for everyone who helped with at least one. Deeds remain permanent Guild-wide milestones.

Founding Members

Heroes carried forward from the pre-Fellowship Guild are recognized as Founding Members. This recognition does not replace or reset any existing character progress.

Stats, Combat Values & Status Effects — Quick Guide

New to RPG stats? These are the numbers Crystal Guild uses to decide how hard you hit, how much punishment you can take, and how your Job performs in battle. Different Jobs favor different stats, so there is no single best stat for every hero.

Quick mental model: STR hits harder · VIT keeps you standing · MAG strengthens spells · SPR resists magic · DEX acts sooner · LCK improves critical hits.

STR — Strength

Raises physical damage. STR also contributes to the maximum AP pool for Jobs that use martial techniques.

VIT — Vitality

Represents physical toughness. Higher VIT reduces damage from physical enemy attacks; Jobs with stronger VIT growth also tend to develop larger HP pools.

MAG — Magic

Raises magical damage and the strength of many healing effects. MAG also contributes to the maximum MP pool.

SPR — Spirit

Represents magical resilience. Higher SPR reduces incoming magical damage and also contributes to maximum MP.

DEX — Dexterity

Helps determine turn order when heroes queue the same kind of action. DEX also contributes to the maximum AP pool.

LCK — Luck

Raises your chance to land a physical critical hit. Some Jobs, equipment and effects can add further critical-hit bonuses.

Derived Combat Values

ATK — Attack

Flat physical power supplied mainly by weapons and equipment. ATK is combined with STR when calculating physical damage.

M.PWR — Magic Power

Flat magical power supplied mainly by caster equipment. Magic Power is combined with MAG when calculating magical damage.

HP — Hit Points

Your health. Damage lowers HP; reaching 0 HP knocks the hero out until they are revived or properly recovered.

MP — Magic Points

A persistent resource spent on magical abilities. MP does not refill automatically after battle; recover it through camp, recovery items or applicable battle recovery.

AP — Action Points

A persistent resource spent on martial and physical techniques. AP does not refill automatically after battle; recover it through camp, AP items or applicable battle recovery.

Not every Job uses every value equally. A Black Mage cares far more about MAG, Magic Power and MP than STR, while a Warrior generally benefits more from STR, ATK, VIT and AP. Hybrid Jobs may use both sides of the system.

Buffs & Tactical Status Effects

Buffs are temporary combat effects applied by abilities, items, equipment effects, or enemy behavior. Their exact strength and duration depend on the source; the descriptions below explain what each status changes in Crystal Guild.

Protect

Reduces incoming damage while active. Stronger Protect effects reduce a larger share of the hit. Defend can provide an even stronger temporary reduction for that round.

Taunt

Makes that hero more likely to be selected by single-target enemy attacks and skills. It raises target-selection weight rather than guaranteeing every attack.

Evasion

Adds a chance to avoid incoming attacks entirely. Evasion from temporary status and applicable equipment effects combine, with a safety cap so a hero cannot become permanently unhittable.

Power Up

Raises outgoing damage. On enemies it increases their offensive pressure; on heroes it strengthens damage dealt while the effect lasts.

Focus

Improves physical critical-hit chance for heroes. Enemies do not make normal critical rolls, so enemy Focus instead increases their outgoing damage pressure.

Haste

Moves actions earlier in the round and increases outgoing damage pressure. Haste does not grant an extra action; enemies still take one normal action under the soft-targeting system.

Regen

Restores HP at the end of each round while the status remains active. The amount depends on the strength of the Regen effect.

Ailments & Harmful Status Effects

Ailments can be inflicted by enemy skills, player abilities, and certain equipment effects. Gear may provide resistance or immunity to specific ailments, and cleansing items can remove them during battle.

Vulnerable

Causes the target to take increased incoming damage. The bonus damage is bounded so Vulnerable cannot scale without limit.

Weaken

Reduces the target's outgoing damage. It affects both heroes and enemies, making their attacks and damaging abilities less threatening.

Slow

Moves actions later in the round and reduces outgoing damage pressure. Slow does not remove a turn; it makes that turn later and weaker.

Stun

The affected hero or enemy loses their action for the round while Stun is active. Smelling Salts can remove Stun from a hero.

Poison

Deals damage at the end of each round based on the target's maximum HP and the strength of the Poison effect. Antidote removes Poison.

Silence

Prevents a hero from using MP-based abilities. AP techniques, Attack, Defend, and applicable items remain available. Echo Herbs remove Silence.

Blind

Gives physical attacks and physical abilities a chance to miss. Magical damage is not affected by Blind. Eye Drops remove it.

Status duration: Battle status displays use entries such as Protect:2 or Poison:3; the number is the remaining rounds. Reapplying a status refreshes it using the stronger magnitude and longer remaining duration. Chronos Tear removes Slow, while Remedy and Panacea broadly clear harmful statuses.

Chocobo Companions

Your registered Chocobo is your companion for distant Expeditions.

Acquisition path: the Fellowship constructs the Chocobo Stable, then you complete A Chocobo of Your Own, purchase a 500 Gil Chocobo Registration Writ, wait one hour for it to activate, and register your bird.

Shared Stable

The Chocobo Stable is a Fellowship LVL 2 project requiring 5,000 Gil and 10 completed Ventures. Once built, it is available Guild-wide.

Personal Quest

Reach Character Lv. 10, complete 10 Ventures, and record at least one C-Rank Hunt clear. Completing the quest awards 250 Gil.

Registration Writ

The Writ costs 500 Gil and takes one hour to validate. The countdown keeps its progress if you leave the Portal and come back later.

Bond Specialization

Registered Chocobos gain Bond XP and have one persistent Expedition specialty derived from their existing trait: Guardian, Scout, Pathfinder, Forager, or Treasure Hunter. The specialty improves at Bond Lv. 5, 10, 15, and 20.

Expedition readiness: you need a registered Chocobo and the completed Fellowship Expedition Board. See Expeditions for the full travel guide.

Use !chocobo, !chocoboquest, !chocobowrit, !registerchocobo <name>, and !chocoboname <name>, or use the Adventurer Portal Chocobo Stable.

Moogle Retainer

Retainer Service

Retainers are personal procurement agents for crafting materials. They do not store equipment, equip gear, or enter combat. Optional targeted procurement and an eight-entry recent-haul record are included.

Dispatch & Return

Open Retainer Service in your Adventurer Portal, choose one unlocked assignment, then choose General mixed haul or a Priority material offered by that route. A priority request costs extra Gil and guarantees one existing normal reward roll for that material. Only one assignment may be active at a time, and the entire haul is committed when the Retainer departs.

Collect & Rank Up

When the timer finishes, collect the secured haul from the Portal. Collection is exactly-once. Completed assignments award Retainer EXP, unlocking longer procurement routes with broader material pools. The Portal records the eight most recent collected hauls. Relic and Artifact materials remain excluded.

Assignments: General prices remain Field Gathering (30m / 100 Gil), Monster Salvage (1h / 250 Gil), Mineral Survey (2h / 500 Gil, Rank 2), Regional Procurement (4h / 900 Gil, Rank 3), and Specialist Acquisition (8h / 1,500 Gil, Rank 4). Priority prices are 125 / 325 / 625 / 1,125 / 1,875 Gil respectively.
Merc Recruitment

Hero Merc Service

Build a permanent Hero roster, then spend Hunt Seals to deploy recruited Heroes on Hunts, Ventures, and Raids. The original six Heroes remain the Guild's founding roster; expansion and Elite Heroes must first be recruited permanently. Elite Mercs are intentionally expensive but provide the strongest specialized assists in the service.

Merc Service uses the same Hunt Seals already earned from Hunts. Hunts already award Hunt Seals; permanent recruitment adds no second Merc currency. Hunt Seals remain the per-assignment deployment fee.
Two separate costs: permanent recruitment unlocks a Hero for your roster. Individual assignments still use the established Hunt Seal contract fee. Recruiting a Hero never consumes Hunt Seals, and deploying a Hero never repeats a permanent Gil recruitment fee.

Hire Heroes

Choose Hunt, Venture, or Raid and spend Hunt Seals to reserve one recruited Hero for that assignment. Each assignment type has its own slot.

Recruitment

Expansion Heroes begin locked. Complete their listed quest objectives or meet the level requirement and pay their one-time Guild contract to recruit them permanently.

Merc Registry

Track the full roster in one place. Unrecruited Heroes remain hidden behind their locked silhouette artwork until their recruitment is complete.

Elite Merc Contracts

Elite Mercs are late-game premium contracts. Their permanent recruitment requires high Character Level, Hunt clears, Dungeon clears, and a large one-time Gil payment. Their deployment fees are also higher than standard Mercs.

Gold Saucer

Gold Saucer — v1.9.0 Alpha 3 title expansion

A Portal-first side hall for low-stakes daily luck, quick attractions, cosmetic prizes, and personal records. Mini Cactpot, Crystal Slots, Monster Toss, Chocobo Racing, and Triple Triad are currently playable.

Manderville Gold Saucer Points (MGP): your first visit grants 500 MGP. After the welcome day, your first Saucer visit each UTC day grants a 125 MGP floor allowance, and every UTC day includes a separate 50 MGP daily reward. MGP is separate from Gil, cannot be purchased with Gil or real money, and does not replace normal Crystal Guild progression.

Mini Cactpot · Daily

Spend 10 MGP to open one persistent daily ticket. One cell is revealed automatically; reveal three more, preview the highlighted rows/columns/diagonals, then choose a line. New tickets reset at 00:00 UTC, and the sum of the three chosen numbers determines your payout.

Crystal Slots · Quick Play

Spend 15 MGP per spin. Three matching symbols pay the strongest reward, while any pair pays a smaller win. Results are committed by the Bot before the Portal runs the reel-stop animation, so visual timing can never change the authoritative outcome.

Monster Toss

Spend 20 MGP for a five-throw game. Pick a target and Safe, Normal, or Power throw style. Payout tiers are 10 / 15 / 30 / 50 / 80 / 120 MGP at 75 / 100 / 250 / 400 / 600 / 900 points, so safer beginner play can earn something while high-risk accuracy pays more. Three games are available per UTC day, and active games persist safely through refreshes or reconnects.

Chocobo Racing

Race your existing registered Chocobo up to three races per UTC day. Manderville Sprint opens at Bond Lv. 1 for 25 MGP, Crystal Circuit at Lv. 5 for 35 MGP, and Golden Gauntlet at Lv. 10 for 50 MGP. Choose Steady, Balanced, or Final Sprint pace; Bond level and favored traits provide modest performance help, while the authoritative six-racer result is still uncertain.

Triple Triad

Build a five-card deck and play on a 3×3 board using the Open ruleset: a higher touching side captures the lower adjacent card. The values printed on each illustrated card face are the authoritative Top/Right/Bottom/Left values. You begin with Chocobo, Moogle, Cactuar, Bomb, and Flan, can play up to five matches per UTC day, and unlock stronger opponents at 3 and 8 career wins. Winning can add a new card to the persistent 15-card collection.

Prize Counter & Challenge Titles

Gold Saucer titles now come from two paths. Prize Counter titles are affordable MGP vanity purchases, while Challenge Titles are earned automatically from Saucer milestones such as jackpots, race wins, high scores, card streaks, and broad attraction play. Titles never add combat stats, Gil, items, or progression power.

Your Saucer Ledger

The Portal tracks MGP earned/spent, net MGP, total games, Mini Cactpot net wins/draws/losses and high rolls, Slot win rate, triple matches, Monster Toss records, Chocobo race records, Triple Triad matches/wins/cards/streak, best payouts, collected prizes, and recent Saucer activity.

Collapsible floor

To keep the Gold Saucer readable, Monster Toss, Chocobo Racing, Triple Triad, Prize Counter, Saucer Ledger, and Recent Activity can be collapsed using the shared finger-pointer controls. Mini Cactpot and Crystal Slots remain permanently open.

Expeditions

Expeditions — v1.2

Take your Chocobo beyond the normal Adventure Board on persistent, multi-stage journeys where resources, choices, exploration, and the Expedition Cache carry from stage to stage.

Unlock: the Guild must complete the Expedition Board, which becomes available at Fellowship LVL 6 + Guild Hall Lv. 1. Each adventurer also needs their own registered Chocobo.

Regions: Emerald Wilds, Ashen Highlands, Mistfen Marshes, Frostveil Reach, or the Astral Scar.

Choose a Region

Emerald Wilds · Lv. 1–12

Exploration-focused forests, rivers, ruins, and overgrown trails. Primary finds include Ochu Vine, Cockatrice Pinion, and Ancient Cloth.

Ashen Highlands · Lv. 8–20

Combat and material gathering across mines, volcanic ravines, and old fortifications. Expect unstable terrain and harder fights.

Mistfen Marshes · Lv. 15–28

Hazard-heavy wetlands and sunken ruins where route risk, status pressure, and preparation matter more.

Frostveil Reach · Lv. 22–38

An endurance region of frozen passes, abandoned strongholds, and bitter conditions that put recovery resources under pressure.

The Astral Scar · Lv. 30+

Late-game dimensional ruins, crystal fractures, rare encounters, and unpredictable Astral instability.

Choose a Route

Scout

3 stages. Shorter commitment with lower overall risk and a reduced reward multiplier.

Journey

5 stages. The balanced standard route for exploration and rewards.

Deep Expedition

8 stages. Longer and more dangerous, with the strongest base reward multiplier.

After completing the planned route, some Expeditions let you push deeper for up to two bonus stages. Returning instead secures what you have already earned.

What Happens During a Journey

Persistent Travel

Your travel and current stage stay saved. You can leave the Portal and return later without losing the Expedition or its pending decision.

Stage Events

Stages may become combat, treasure, resource nodes, hazards, discoveries, travelers, Chocobo events, camp opportunities, or rare encounters.

Resources Carry Forward

Your HP, MP, AP, and consumed supplies persist across the run. Expedition Camp opportunities help you recover before deciding whether to continue.

Route Choices

Some stages ask you to choose a path or response. Choices can alter danger, rewards, discoveries, and the next stage rather than acting as flavor-only prompts.

Expedition Cache, Return & Defeat

Expedition Cache

Gil, materials, and other Expedition rewards stay in a temporary Cache while you travel. They are not permanently banked until you make it home.

Safe Return

You may return to the Guild early when offered the option. A normal safe return banks the full current Expedition Cache.

Chocobo Rescue

If the run ends in defeat or combat retreat, your Chocobo brings you home and preserves most of the Cache instead of wiping the entire journey.

Exploration, Objectives & Regional Bosses

Each region tracks persistent exploration. Reaching 25%, 50%, 75%, and 100% advances its objective chain and opens progressively deeper regional content.

25% / 50%

Early and mid-region objectives establish landmarks, routes, and discoveries. Progress remains permanently recorded even between separate Expeditions.

75% · Boss Contract

The region's special Boss Expedition unlocks: Elder Ochu, Inferno Drake, Malboro Sovereign, Frost Behemoth, or Rift Wyrm.

Rare Encounters

Higher exploration can add dangerous regional variants and rarer opportunities to the normal event pool.

100% · Region Mastery

Mastery is permanent and awards the region's achievement/title plus a small regional Expedition bonus. Mastered regions remain fully playable.

Expedition Atlas & Legendary Contracts

Expedition Atlas

Your five regional surveys now combine into one overall Atlas rank. The Adventure Board shows overall exploration plus total stages, completed runs, Regional Boss clears, mastered regions, and unlocked Legendary routes.

Cartography Ranks

Overall exploration advances through Guild Scout → Trailblazer → Pathfinder → Surveyor → Cartographer. Master all five regions to earn the final Master Cartographer distinction.

Legendary Contracts

After a region reaches 100% Mastery and its Regional Boss has been defeated at least once, that region gains a repeatable Legendary Contract: five stages, stronger boss pressure, higher rewards, and doubled guaranteed regional boss materials.

All-Region Reward

Mastering all five Expedition regions unlocks the No Blank Spots Left achievement and the equippable Master Cartographer title. Your existing exploration progress counts toward this automatically.

Regional Discovery

Permanent Landmarks

Every region now contains four named landmarks that become permanently charted as exploration rises. The Expedition Atlas shows your total landmark count, while the selected region reveals descriptions for places you have already mapped.

Regional Event Pools

Normal resource, treasure, hazard, traveler, and Chocobo stages now use writing tied to the selected region. Emerald Wilds feels like overgrown exploration; Ashen Highlands leans into mines and Magitek debris; Mistfen into fog and drowned ruins; Frostveil into endurance; and the Astral Scar into unstable reality.

Rare Regional Events

Dungeon Cache & retreat

Clearing a Dungeon banks the full Cache plus its completion bonus. A voluntary retreat banks 50%; defeat recovers 25%. The deeper you go, the more of the temporary Cache you are choosing to risk.

FAQ & Glossary

Quick answers to common questions.

Do I lose XP or gear when I get KO'd?
No. KO blocks combat until recovery, but there is no permanent death, XP loss, gear loss or durability system.
Why can't I Venture?
Common reasons: the relevant normal or Solo Venture cooldown is active, you're resting, you're KO'd, or you're already involved in the active channel encounter. Solo Venture uses its own 7-minute cooldown and does not consume the normal Venture cooldown. An active Solo Venture remains waiting on the Adventure Board until you act, return, or retreat.
Does changing Jobs reset anything?
No. Character Level persists and every Job keeps its own JP/Job Level. Your current HP is clamped to the new Job's valid maximum.
What is JP?
Job Points . JP levels the Job you are currently using. Job Levels unlock passive abilities and a Mastery at JLv. 50.
What are Hunt Seals?
A Hunt currency earned primarily from named marks. Spend them at !sealshop and on some crafting/Relic costs.
How does bad-luck protection work?
Some rare drops become guaranteed after enough unsuccessful attempts. When the item finally drops, that protection resets.
What is the difference between Character Level and Job Level?
Character Level (1–99) is permanent overall power and unlock progression. Job Levels (1–50 each) are independent training tracks for specific Jobs.
Can I join an encounter if I didn't start it?
Yes. During the encounter rally use !join or !venture . Joining consumes your own Venture cooldown.
What is a World Event?
World Events are rare stream-only Notorious Monster encounters. They do not start a Raid. A World Event can scale up to 10 levels above the Guild's highest current hero, and joined players fight it through the normal live battle controls.
What do the combat button colors mean?
Red and orange mark offensive actions, green marks healing and recovery, purple marks support actions, and blue marks defensive actions. The action name is always shown too, so color is only an extra visual cue.
How do mobile battle controls work?
During combat, a compact quick bar stays with the fight so you can see enemy HP, your HP and MP or AP, and use Attack, Ability, Item, or Defend without scrolling back to the main battle cards. Ability and Item choices open in a bounded drawer just above the bar with their own scroll area, while the battle page remains visible and scrollable outside it.
How does the Moogle Trading Post work?
The Trading Post is the player market. Listings are fixed-price, last 48 hours, charge a 2% listing fee and a 5% sale tax, and each player can have up to 24 active listings. Quest items, Relics, explicit untradeables, and your last equipped copy of gear cannot be listed.
What does the Moogle Emporium Annex add?
A completed Annex adds 2 Weapon slots, 4 Armor or Accessory slots, and 6 Item slots to each personal Emporium rotation. When enough eligible stock exists, that raises the rotation from 9 choices to 21.
Do new achievement Titles unlock if I already met the requirement?
Yes. Existing progress is checked automatically. If you already satisfy a newly added achievement, its reward Title can be granted without repeating the activity. Once an achievement is earned, it stays earned even if a reversible value such as held Gil later drops.
What counts as a monster defeat for projects and objectives?
Any valid Bestiary monster kill can count toward a broad Monsters Defeated requirement, including normal encounters, Hunts, Ventures, Expeditions, Dungeons, and World Events. Requirements that name a specific activity, such as Hunts won or Raid clears, still require that exact activity.
Can I instantly heal instead of camping?
Yes. A Tent restores full HP and ends camp immediately. X-Potion also fully heals; Phoenix Down revives from KO.
What do boss tiers mean?
Primal Boss → World Boss → High Boss → Superboss → Ultimate Superboss. Higher tiers generally expect stronger guilds, larger HP pools and harsher raid tuning.
